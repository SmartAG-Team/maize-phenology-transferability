Fig. 1. Overall study workflow. Qualified native dates and weather enter spatially held, source-only fitting. Four branches address pooled performance, local history, annual-temperature transfer and weather sensitivity. Transfer includes the 40-site pooled control; local updating uses five date-offset models and the frozen 80-site coverage fit (draw 17). Weather fits use shared-station grouping and four source years/site. Paired outcomes are common within branches; budgets and cohorts differ between branches. Annual temperature defines transfer zones; growing-season climate defines blend support. Intervals condition on fitted models; tests assume supplied weather.

Fig. 7. Geographic distribution of BBCH-87 transfer penalties. (a–c) Coldest-zone source fits for nonlinear T, MLP and the support blend, respectively. (d–f) Warmest-zone source fits for the same three specifications. Each value subtracts that model’s target-zone-trained error from its source-zone-trained error on identical native outcomes, then averages the three source draws and equally weights monitoring sites within a native weather cell. All source fits use 40 sites and eight historical years/site in five spatial folds; targets are native 2011–2025 BBCH-87 observations. Positive values indicate excess loss, and negative values favour foreign-zone training. Black crosses identify invalid source or target-reference dates included with 366-day loss. Colours saturate beyond ±30 d; the numerical files retain exact penalties, completion and native observed-year/label support. Points are recorded native weather-cell centres; blank areas are not interpolated. All panels share ETRS89-LAEA Europe (EPSG:3035) and geographic extent. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m. These are descriptive sampled-cell patterns, not tests of significant geographic clusters or causal temperature effects.

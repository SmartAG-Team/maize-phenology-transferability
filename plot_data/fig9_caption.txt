Fig. 9. Comparison of station-observed DWD weather with model-based gridded ERA5-Land reanalysis weather (0.1°). (a) Daily (Tmin+Tmax)/2. (b) Diurnal temperature range (Tmax−Tmin). Colours show counts in 1 °C bins; dashed lines denote equality. A native weather-cell/calendar-day pair is counted once despite overlapping sowing-origin windows. Different cells can share a DWD station, so cells are not independent meteorological replicates. DWD extremes are quality-controlled, explicitly 00–24 UTC and geographically qualified; ERA5-Land uses its native UTC grid aggregates. Temperature differences include station/grid representativeness. Native day-level comparison data and exact binned counts are retained separately.

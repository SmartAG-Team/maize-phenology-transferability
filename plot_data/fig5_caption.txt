Fig. 5. Accuracy and distributions of gains from historical local observations. (a) MAE at 0, 1, 3 and 5 pre-2011 local years; shading gives 95% target-block intervals. (b) Violin distributions of paired MAE reduction from zero to five historical years; positive values indicate improvement. Each model uses 5,323 matched site–endpoint pairs at 822 sites. Density weights are equal across seven native endpoints, then across sites within each endpoint. Gaussian kernels use Scott bandwidth in untransformed days, equal maximum widths and the full observed ranges. The signed asinh horizontal scale (2-day linear width) expands the centre while retaining all tails; ticks remain in days. Black circles/whiskers show equal-head mean reductions and 95% target-block intervals; white diamonds show weighted medians. All models share 2011–2025 targets, source-derived shrinkage and historical-year budgets; missing heads receive no update. Five spatial folds use 80 coverage-selected source sites, eight years/site and draw 17. Intervals condition on fitted models; densities do not imply independent site–endpoint replicates.

Fig. 5. Accuracy after supplying a limited history at previously held locations. Budgets of 0, 1, 3 and 5 select the latest available native site-years ending by 2010; the 2011–2025 evaluation population remains identical across budgets and models: 49,462 labels at 822 sites. Every native head uses a median of yearly mean date residuals, shrunk by n/(n+kappa), where n counts available head-years and kappa is the same source-derived value used by all displayed date-offset models. Missing native heads receive no update. Nonlinear-T history predictions are recomputed with actual weather prefixes ending by 2010. Source fits use the frozen 80-site coverage design, eight years/site and source draw 17 in five spatial folds. Shading shows 95% target-block intervals conditional on fitted models. This adapted-site cohort is smaller than the zero-shot network benchmark; its absolute errors are not a comparison of weather products or an estimate of operational forecast skill.

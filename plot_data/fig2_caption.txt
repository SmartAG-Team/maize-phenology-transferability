Fig. 2. Geographic support of the qualified maize phenology study. (a) Distinct observed calendar years per native monitoring/weather cell during 1981–2025; repeated stages do not add years. (b) Five cold-to-warm annual-temperature zones from complete 1981–2010 reference climate. (c) Availability of the local-history and paired-weather cohorts among the 1,029 later-period evaluation sites. Local only, weather only, both and neither are disjoint eligibility classes, not model-performance categories. Panels (a, b) retain all 2,232 qualified German monitoring-site groups; local and weather cohorts contain 822 and 268 later sites, respectively. Points are recorded native weather-cell centres rather than surveyed field positions. No interpolation fills unobserved areas. All panels share ETRS89-LAEA Europe (EPSG:3035), extent and one geographic scale bar. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m. Observed-year counts and ranges, native-label/unit counts and overlapping cohort memberships accompany the plotting data.

Fig. S1. Absolute errors underlying the annual-temperature transfer experiment. (a) T. (b) Nonlinear T. (c) Residual RF. (d) Residual MLP. (e) Causal thermal Transformer. (f) Climate-support blend. Zones 1–5 are cold-to-warm quintiles of complete 1981–2010 January–December temperature within the German exact-date network. Each source fit uses 40 sites and eight distinct years/site ending by 2010; three source draws and five spatial folds share identical 2011–2025 target observations. Cells show equal-head, site-year-balanced errors in days. These values describe absolute accuracy; Fig. 6 separately adjusts for the target zone’s own trained-model error. All seven native heads receive equal weight. Invalid dates receive a 366-day loss. Exact native definitions remain unchanged; these are retrospective German-network comparisons, not causal temperature responses. Confidence intervals, stage support and source sampling details accompany the plotting data.

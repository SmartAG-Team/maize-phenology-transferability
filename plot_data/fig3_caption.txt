Fig. 3. Native-stage performance and the opposing effects of learned predictions. (a) Penalised errors for seven source-native endpoints under the earlier 80-site random-source design. (b) Gross error reduction (positive, solid) and gross error increase (negative, hatched) relative to nonlinear T on identical individual observations. (c) Net error reduction, equal to gross benefit minus gross harm, with a separate signed difference scale; positive values favour the candidate. Whiskers in (b) and (c) are paired 95% target-block intervals after averaging source draws. The models in (b) and (c) are compared with nonlinear T; RF/MLP corrections themselves are trained from T teachers. All panels use identical 2011–2025 targets, five held spatial folds, eight source years/site and three source draws. Observations are averaged within site-years, years within sites and sites within heads; (b) and (c) weight seven heads equally. Invalid dates retain a 366-day loss. Native-stage definitions remain separate, including BBCH 87. Table 2 supplies overall accuracy; (a) shows its stage distribution.

Fig. 10. Sensitivity to station-observed DWD weather and model-based gridded ERA5-Land reanalysis weather (0.1°) on identical native phenology observations. (a) Error change after substituting the other weather product with fitted parameters and normalization held fixed; the reference is each fit’s own training product. (b) DWD-minus-ERA5 error after fitting and evaluating each model on its respective product. Negative values in (b) favour the DWD-specific fit. Every product uses the same 40 source sites, four distinct 2001–2010 years/site, source validation budgets and native 2011–2024 targets in five spatial folds and three source draws. All 367 native input days require quality-controlled DWD temperature extremes with explicit 00–24 UTC observation windows and time-valid station positions within 10 km of the native weather-cell centre. Connected source block/station groups remain whole in inner validation and teacher fitting. Whiskers are 95% paired intervals from connected target station/spatial-block resamples, conditional on fitted models. Native-block-only intervals are retained as a sensitivity. The comparison includes weather-product and station/grid representativeness differences; DWD station weather is not assumed to be exact field weather.

Fig. 10. Distributions of weather-product effects on common maize dates. (a) Paired error changes under fixed-parameter weather swaps, relative to each fit’s own product. (b) DWD-minus-ERA5 errors after product-specific refitting; negative values favour the DWD-specific fit. Each distribution contains 1,698 site–endpoint pairs at 268 sites, after averaging three source draws. Density weights are equal across seven native endpoints, then across sites within endpoints. Gaussian kernels use Scott bandwidth in untransformed days and equal maximum widths. The signed asinh axis (2-day linear width) retains all tails; ticks are days. Black circles/whiskers retain the mean contrasts and conditional 95% intervals from connected station/spatial-block resampling; white diamonds show weighted medians. Matched source budgets are 40 sites × four 2001–2010 years in five folds; targets span 2011–2024. DWD station observations and ERA5-Land reanalysis (0.1°) have different spatial supports. DWD is not assumed to be exact field weather.

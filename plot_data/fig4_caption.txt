Fig. 4. Geographic distribution of held-site prediction errors. (a, b) T and nonlinear-T MAE for flowering onset (BBCH 61). (c) MLP-minus-nonlinear-T error at BBCH 61. (d, e) T and nonlinear-T MAE at BBCH 87. (f) MLP-minus-nonlinear-T error at BBCH 87. Negative differences favour MLP. Each square is a recorded native weather-cell centre; monitoring-site/head errors sharing a cell receive equal weight after within-site year averaging. All models share the same native targets and the earlier 80-site random-source design, eight historical years/site and three source draws in five spatial folds. Endpoints retain their own observed support. No interpolation fills unsampled areas. Black crosses indicate invalid or unresolved predictions contributing a 366-day loss. Colour saturates above 30 d for MAE and beyond ±10 d for differences; exact values and observed-year/label support accompany the plotting data. All panels share ETRS89-LAEA Europe (EPSG:3035) and geographic extent. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m. Positions do not represent surveyed crop-field GPS.

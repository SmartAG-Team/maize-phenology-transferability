Fig. 8. Transfer along the annual-temperature gradient. (a) Unique target-site support. (b) Penalised MAE. (c) Excess loss relative to target-zone training. Negative horizontal differences denote colder targets. Off-diagonal comparisons use fixed distance bins and actual within-bin mean distances. Native outcomes are year/site/head balanced; source draws and conditions are averaged within target-site/head/bin before site averaging. Shading gives conditional 95% target-block intervals; incomplete seven-head support remains undefined. A site can enter multiple bins, so counts are not additive. The far-cold bin contains nine sites. Invalid dates incur 366-day loss. The two directions retain different target populations and do not identify causal warming or cooling effects.

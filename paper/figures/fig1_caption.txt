Fig. 1. Workflow for evaluating maize phenology models in the German monitoring network. (a) Model performance using all eligible historical training data. (b) Local calibration with historical observations. (c) Transfer among annual-temperature zones and a pooled training control. (d) Weather-product substitution and recalibration. Model training, selection, residual corrections and normalization use pre-2011 data. Paired comparisons share target observations within each experiment; training budgets and cohorts differ among experiments. Local calibration uses five models and one fixed training sample. Annual temperature defines transfer zones; April–September climate determines blend weights.

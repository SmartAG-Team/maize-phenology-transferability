Fig. 7. Transfer errors at physiological maturity (BBCH 87) within Germany. (a–c) Coldest-zone training for nonlinear T, MLP and the support blend, respectively. (d–f) Warmest-zone training for the same models. Excess MAE (d) subtracts the error after target-zone training on common valid 2011–2025 predictions; positive values indicate higher error. Values average three training samples and monitoring sites within weather grid cells. Each fit uses 40 sites and eight historical years per site. Crosses mark cells with invalid predictions from either model in the contrast; failures receive no numerical error penalty. Colours saturate beyond ±30 d. Only centres inside Germany are displayed; summaries retain the full cohort. Blank areas are unsampled. Panels share EPSG:3035, extent and one scale bar. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m.

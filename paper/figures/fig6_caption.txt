Fig. 6. Transfer errors among annual-temperature zones. (a) T. (b) Nonlinear T. (c) Residual RF. (d) Residual MLP. (e) Thermal Transformer. (f) Support blend. Zones 1–5 are quintiles of 1981–2010 mean annual temperature, ordered from coldest to warmest. Cells show excess MAE (d): error after training in the source zone minus error after training in the target zone, using common valid 2011–2025 predictions. Positive values indicate higher error; diagonals are zero. Each fit uses 40 sites and eight historical years per site, with three training samples in each of five spatial folds. Stages receive equal weight; prediction failures are reported separately.

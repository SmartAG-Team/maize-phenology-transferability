Fig. 10. Effects of weather inputs on prediction errors. (a, b) Changes in MAE and bias after substituting weather without refitting, relative to each model’s original product. (c, d) DWD-minus-ERA5 changes in MAE and bias after product-specific refitting. Points and lines show paired means and conditional 95% confidence intervals on shared valid observations. Positive bias changes indicate later predictions. Training uses 40 sites and four years per site; test observations span 2011–2024. Resampling accounts for shared stations and spatial blocks.

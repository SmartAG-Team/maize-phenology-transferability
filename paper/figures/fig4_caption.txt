Fig. 4. Geographic distribution of prediction errors within Germany. (a, b) T and nonlinear-T MAE at flowering onset (BBCH 61), respectively. (c) MLP-minus-nonlinear-T MAE at flowering onset. (d–f) Corresponding quantities at physiological maturity (BBCH 87). Negative differences favour MLP. Squares show weather grid-cell averages; crosses mark cells with invalid or unresolved predictions, which are excluded from MAE. Models use common 2011–2025 targets and all eligible historical training observations; paired differences use common valid predictions. Colours saturate above 30 d for MAE and beyond ±10 d for differences. Only centres inside Germany are displayed; summaries retain the full cohort. Blank areas are unsampled. Panels share EPSG:3035, extent and one 200-km scale bar. Boundaries: Natural Earth 1:50m.

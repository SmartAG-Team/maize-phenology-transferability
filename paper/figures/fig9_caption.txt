Fig. 9. DWD station and ERA5-Land reanalysis temperatures (0.1° grid). (a) Daily mean temperature, (Tmin + Tmax)/2. (b) Diurnal temperature range, Tmax − Tmin. Colours count distinct grid-cell/calendar-day pairs in 1 °C bins; dashed lines indicate equality. Overlapping weather windows from different sowing dates are counted once. Some grid cells share DWD stations. Both products use qualified 00–24 UTC days; differences also reflect the spatial representation of station and grid temperatures.

Fig. 8. Prediction performance across climatic distance. (a) Sites with valid nonlinear-T comparisons. (b) MAE. (c) Bias. The horizontal axis is target minus training-median annual temperature; negative values indicate colder targets. Points aggregate fixed distance bins, and shading shows conditional 95% confidence intervals. Counts are not additive because sites can occur in several comparisons. The far-cold bin contains nine sites. The two directions involve different target populations.

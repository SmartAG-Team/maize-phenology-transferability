## Deutscher Wetterdienst

Source: Deutscher Wetterdienst (DWD), Climate Data Center (CDC), phenological observations and daily station weather. Native identifiers, reporting definitions, observation windows and quality flags remain in the scientific evidence. Published tables and examples are selected or derived records, rather than an unmodified official archive.

The CDC terms dated May2024 specify CC BY4.0: https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/Terms_of_use.pdf . Original source: https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/ . Attribution and modifications remain explicit.

## Copernicus / ECMWF

The examples and climate summaries contain modified Copernicus Climate Change Service information(2026), processed through the documented ERA5-Land and AgERA5 products. The European Commission and ECMWF bear no responsibility for subsequent use of these derived data.

ERA5-Land daily aggregates: https://developers.google.com/earth-engine/datasets/catalog/ECMWF_ERA5_LAND_DAILY_AGGR . Product source: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-land . Its applicable source licence and attribution terms remain in force.

AgERA5: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/sis-agrometeorological-indicators . The native model-weather source includes the Earth Engine collection projects/climate-engine-pro/assets/ce-ag-era5-v2/daily. AgERA5 local-day data and ERA5-Land UTC data remain distinct products; they are not relabelled or silently interchanged.
